ReagentRoute

Cheapest path to level a WoW Classic profession, priced against live Auction House data.

If a reagent has no Auction House listing but is a raw material you could gather yourself, checking the matching profession treats it as free instead of blocking the recipe from the plan. Gathered reagents are marked in the results and link to their Wowhead page for farming locations.

Off by default, and deliberately conservative when on: only the first N units of each crafted item across your whole plan (not per craft) are valued at today's Auction House price, minus Blizzard's 5% cut on a sale. Everything beyond that cap still falls back to the guaranteed vendor price shown elsewhere — assuming you can sell unlimited duplicates at one live listing's price isn't realistic and produces nonsense numbers.
